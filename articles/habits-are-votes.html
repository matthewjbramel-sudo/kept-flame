Habits Are Votes

Forget goals. Goals are about the finish line; habits are about the direction. And the deepest way to understand a habit isn't as a behavior — it's as a vote. Every time you act, you're casting a ballot for the kind of person you are. Write for an hour: one vote for writer. Skip the workout: one vote for someone who skips. No single vote decides the election. But the count compounds, and the count is always running.

This reframe — identity-based habits — changes the question. Not "how do I stick to the diet?" but "what would a healthy person do here?" Not "how do I write a book?" but "what does a writer do on a Tuesday?" The behavior follows the identity, and the identity is built one vote at a time.

Why votes beat goals

Goals have an endpoint, and endpoints invite stopping. Hit the target weight and the diet ends; finish the marathon and the running stops. Votes never end — there's always another ballot, another Tuesday. The person voting writer doesn't stop when the book ships. The identity is the engine; the goals were just milestones.

Goals also make every off day a failure. Miss the target and the story becomes I failed. Votes make off days data — one ballot among hundreds. You don't lose an election over a single vote. You lose it by stopping voting.

The Tuesday-morning version

Name the identity. Not the goal — the person. Writer, athlete, learner, the one who keeps promises. Write it down. This is who the votes are for.

Shrink the ballot. The vote has to be castable on your worst day. Not "work out for an hour" — "put on the shoes." Not "write a chapter" — "open the document." A small vote cast beats a large vote skipped, because the count cares about consistency, not size.

Never miss twice. One missed vote is noise. Two is the start of a new pattern — votes for the other candidate. The rule isn't perfection; it's recovery speed.

Plato's charioteer from Book One doesn't win by one heroic drive. He wins by driving daily — every day a vote for reason over appetite. The election is always open. Cast today's ballot.
